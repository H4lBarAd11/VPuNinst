VPuNinst is a small Mac app for the VPNs of the institutions you belong to, built on openconnect. It signs you in with each institution’s single sign-on in its own window, starts the tunnels with Touch ID, keeps them up, and can run several side by side.

The installer asks for your administrator password once, and sets up five things:

  • VPuNinst, in Applications.
  • Its helper, in /Library/PrivilegedHelperTools. It runs as root and can do only three things without your password: connect to an institution on your list, disconnect, and undo what a dead tunnel left behind.
  • Its tunnel script, beside the helper, which sets up each tunnel’s routes and DNS without touching your network’s own settings.
  • openconnect, built by VPuNinst from its published source with OpenSSL, in a folder only root can change. Nothing else needs installing.
  • A sudoers rule that lets you run the helper’s three steps without a password. The app asks for Touch ID instead. Adding an institution to the list still takes your password.

The README explains what that rule trades. VPuNinst is an independent project, not affiliated with any institution or VPN vendor; the next page is its disclaimer, which you are asked to agree to.
